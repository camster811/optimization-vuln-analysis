## Decisions and evidence inspected

1. **Retain the ten-family final design and source scope.** Keep five pairs per CWE, six interprocedural and four intraprocedural pairs, and the declared original-variant/O0–O3/symbol matrix. The two pilot families demonstrate multi-function extraction and optimization handling, but cannot establish diversity of ten unselected families. Final diversity is governed by the existing ranked, source-only eligibility procedure, one pair per family, and two structural strata. Do not expand the matrix or replace difficult cases on the basis of these two families' predictions. Record residual template similarity during Phase 8 source review. Retain all assigned cells despite elimination, unavailable inputs, or poor predictions.

2. **Retain `ghidra-inferred-stack-bounds-v2` as an explicitly limited heuristic comparator.** The earlier user-authorized amendment permits inferred stack extents and imperfect predictions. Existing independent native/Ghidra fixtures exercise direct caller/helper writes and O0–O3 stores. The 36 stack fixtures produced 24 classifications: 15 correct and nine false negatives, with 12 abstentions and no false positives. The pilot stack paths all abstain; the heap paths supply classifications at higher optimization levels. These are measured limitations, not reasons to redefine the oracle or exclude stack cases. The decision gate concerns generic operation and explicit support limits, not a minimum accuracy or coverage threshold. No testcase-specific summaries or post-pilot classifier changes are introduced. Claims must describe a bounded heuristic baseline, never a sound or comprehensive static analyzer.

3. **Retain `public-bundle-llm-v1`, the pinned 14B Q5_K_M model, and the measured 8,192-token runtime.** Keep the full 4,096-token completion reservation, frozen prompt/schema, seeds 4101–4103, sampler chain, timeout, and schedule. All 96 pilot payloads were tokenized before completing the pilot. Twenty-six public bundles fit; six do not. The runtime was exercised with full GPU offload and flash attention. No larger context's hardware feasibility is assumed. No prompt, sampler, model, or validator change is selected from pilot answer content.

4. **Make exact token eligibility shared.** For both predictors, `context_unavailable` overrides the prediction's scoring availability whenever the complete prompt/template plus completion reserve exceeds 8,192 tokens. Retain all such adjudicated cells in the primary denominator. Preserve raw Phase 5 predictions and report the common-input subset separately. This overlay implements the predeclared shared-context rule; it does not modify public records or erase already obtained baseline outputs.

5. **Retain strict failure and recovery rules.** A dispatched request is never retried because it times out, crashes, has invalid evidence/JSON, or reaches its length limit. Completed attempts from Phase 6 remain part of this pilot. Two matching valid labels win, including two uncertain labels; some valid votes without agreement abstain; zero valid votes are unavailable. Median confidence includes all valid votes and does not weight them. The full pilot archive retains requests, responses, dispatch chronology, usage, timings, and failures. LLM answers are not assumed bitwise reproducible.

6. **Freeze the prospective evaluator.** Exact CWE labels define primary correctness. Abstentions and unavailable cells are never correct. Unresolved binary truth is excluded from binary-label scores, but remains in inventory and source-label recovery. Alert metrics treat wrong-CWE vulnerable answers as alerts; no-alert reasons distinguish safe answers, abstentions, and unavailable outputs. Optimization and symbol contrasts match cells and retain failed predictions when both truths are resolved. Family-cluster bootstrap uses PCG64 seed 20261019, 10,000 replicates and shared multiplicities; each replicate draws the actual number of selected families (two for this pilot, ten for the planned final set). Percentiles use linear interpolation and disclose undefined replicates. Pilot intervals describe two development families only, not the final effect size.

7. **Preserve and disclose localization and adjudication assistance.** Private mapping localizes the selected entry; public traversal follows binary relationships. Final oracle review must finish before final predictors. Existing pilot review was performed by Codex without an independent human adjudicator. No independent reviewer or unrecorded review hours are claimed. The optimized oracle remains unchanged. Public extraction does not establish complete recovery in arbitrary binaries.

8. **Keep development exposure separate from final eligibility.** Both pilot families and all independent method-development fixtures remain development-only. No additional Juliet family is used during Phase 7. The existing source-use provenance review applies before final assignment; unknown prior exposure is grounds for development-only treatment, not an assumption of non-use. Candidate-table enumeration is not predictor exposure. The experiment cannot establish absence of Juliet from LLM pretraining.

9. **Separate method lock from final campaign freeze.** The method lock binds selection policy, build/context/predictor implementation and policies, schemas, model/template/runtime configuration, scoring, contrasts, missingness, and supporting pilot evidence. The local timestamp is not an independent timestamped deposit. A final server may have a new PID, but must match the locked computational configuration and receive new process evidence. Phase 8 may add final orchestration and input manifests; changes to locked methods require an explicit amendment. No final inference is authorized by Phase 7. The original configuration's pending marker is superseded by a verified `provenance/method-lock.json`, without rewriting earlier hash-bound artifacts.

## Acceptance criterion

Proceed to final preparation after all 32 pilot observations have terminal three-slot LLM outcomes, the baseline replays for all 32, both methods are scored with the shared gate, deterministic build/extraction artifacts repeat in a clean workspace, the complete Ghidra-enabled regression suite passes, and the machine-readable method lock verifies. The known abstentions, token exclusions, heuristic errors, and lack of independent human adjudication are accepted limitations of this comparator study. If any technical gate fails, do not create the lock or assign final cases until it is resolved and documented.
